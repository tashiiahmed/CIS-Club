About Us

Building Tomorrow's Tech Leaders

Our Mission

To empower and inspire students in Computer & Information Systems by fostering a collaborative community that promotes learning, innovation, and professional growth through workshops, projects, networking, and mentorship. We strive to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry practice, preparing our members for successful careers in technology.

Our History

2018

Foundation

CIS Club was founded by a group of passionate students with a vision to build a thriving community of tech enthusiasts. What started as a small study group quickly grew into a full-fledged organization.

2020

Growth & Expansion

Expanded membership to 100+ members and launched our first annual hackathon. Established partnerships with local tech companies and introduced mentorship programs.

2022

Innovation & Partnerships

Established partnerships with leading tech companies and launched advanced workshops covering AI, machine learning, and cloud computing. Introduced industry speaker series.

2024

Excellence & Recognition

Reached 500+ members and completed 100+ projects. Recognized as one of the top student organizations on campus. Launched career placement assistance program.
