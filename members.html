Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join CIS Club?

Any student enrolled at our university with an interest in computer science, information systems, or technology is welcome to join regardless of their major or experience level.

When does membership start?

Your membership begins immediately after approval. You'll receive a confirmation email with next steps and access to our member portal.

Can I upgrade my membership later?

Yes! You can upgrade from Basic to Premium or Lifetime membership at any time. Contact us for upgrade options.

Is the membership fee refundable?

Premium membership fees are refundable within the first two weeks. Lifetime memberships are non-refundable but transferable.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and campus cash. Payment instructions will be sent after your application is approved.

How do I stay updated on events?

Members receive weekly email updates, and we post all events on our website and social media channels. Premium members get priority notifications.
